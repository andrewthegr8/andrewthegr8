About

I build robotic systems and prove they work on real hardware.

I'm a robotics systems engineer with a concurrent B.S./M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Cal Poly. My work lives where control theory meets embedded software and physical hardware — state estimation, real-time control, multi-microcontroller communication, and the debugging it takes to make all of it behave on a real machine. I care about reliable motion, honest measurement, and understanding a system from the sensor counts up to the trajectory.
