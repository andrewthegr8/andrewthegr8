Andrew M. Jones — Robotics Systems Engineer

Embedded systems integration, real-time control, and hardware/software debugging. Experienced in optimizing system latency, developing multi-microcontroller architectures, and validating performance through first-principles analysis.

Selected projects

Computer-Vision Pose Estimation — M.S. thesis (solo)

  • Built an overhead-camera localization system giving a mobile robot absolute world-frame pose — fisheye calibration, ArUco detection, and an iterative PnP solve, with an original analytical world-coordinate derivation.
  • Designed a four-MCU pipeline (Raspberry Pi → ESP32 → ESP32 → STM32) over SPI, ESP-NOW, and UART; profiled it to ~87 ms end-to-end.
  • Validated against ground truth at 2.6 mm average accuracy.

4.5-DOF Color-Sorting Robotic Arm — ME 507 (team of 3)

  • Owned the motion subsystem and its electronics: selected the TMC429 motion-control chips and stepper drivers and contributed to the custom STM32H7 controller PCB.
  • Wrote the TMC429 SPI driver, radians-to-steps command layer, and a waypoint-based trajectory engine; debugged multi-day SPI bring-up on the oscilloscope.

Autonomous Mobile Robot — ME 405 (solo)

  • Built an observer-based state estimator (7-state, RK4) and a custom heading-and-speed control law in MicroPython on an STM32.
  • Cut course time from ~30 s to ~11 s — fastest of 12 teams — using a live Bluetooth telemetry and plotting toolchain to drive the tuning.

3-DOF Print-Harvesting Robot — ME 423 (programming lead)

  • Wrote the full embedded control stack on a Raspberry Pi: cubic-spline trajectories, per-motor PI/PID, and telemetry for a five-motor, 21-waypoint harvest sequence.
  • Diagnosed unstable motor behavior to a software-PWM timing mismatch on the oscilloscope and fixed it with hardware-timed (DMA) PWM via pigpio.

Additional — Autonomous Vehicle Programming (AI)

  • Trained and deployed YOLO object-recognition models in a ROS2 environment on scale autonomous vehicles for line-following and sign recognition; worked with LiDAR and ultrasonic sensors; tested in simulation before deployment.
